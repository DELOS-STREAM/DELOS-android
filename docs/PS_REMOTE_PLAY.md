# PlayStation Remote Play

## Purpose

DELOS's own implementation of the PlayStation Remote Play protocol (PS5 first, PS4 kept in the code), written from the
clean-room specification in [research/ps-remote-play-protocol.md](research/ps-remote-play-protocol.md). It lives on the branch
`feature/25-ps-remote-play` and is not merged to `main` until the whole feature is stable (owner, 2026-11-21).

## Scope

Covered: discovery, wake, PSN sign-in, registration with the console PIN, session init and ctrl, the Senkusha network test,
the Takion stream connection with BIG/BANG and the stream keys, video reassembly with Reed-Solomon recovery and loss recovery,
Opus audio, the microphone, controller input with DualSense haptics as rumble and trigger effects to a wired DualSense, the
shell UI (prototype F in `build/ps-pairing-proto/`), PlayStation settings, reconnecting after silence, a session kept while
the app is in the background, and the stream in DELOS's own stream activity (decoder, SGSR upscalers, frame pacing, overlay,
stream menu, controller handling). Not covered: internet play (#126, later); more than one controller (#123: the protocol
announces one controller and its feedback packets carry no controller index); DualSense voice-coil haptics as audio.

## Behavior

1. The console is found by the UDP search on 9302 (PS5) and 987 (PS4). The answer's `system-version` and protocol version
   select the target (`PsTarget`).
2. Registration, session init and ctrl use TCP/UDP 9295 whatever `host-request-port` reports (spec 5.9 (3); a PS5 on firmware
   14230000 reports 997).
3. Takion DATA and DATA_ACK chunks are authenticated with the GMAC but not encrypted; AV payloads, feedback and microphone
   packets are encrypted at key position + 16 (spec 5.9).
4. The PSN account id comes from Sony's sign-in page in a WebView (`PsOAuth`); it is kept for the next pairing.
5. The constants extracted from Sony's client ship in the app (owner, 2026-11-21: open source, not sold). `PsConstants.kt`
   is generated by `tools/ps/gen_constants.py`, which checks every table against the checksums of the constants document.
6. Start screen: the `PC | PlayStation` tab row in the status bar's line (the Settings tab component, L1 / R1 at its sides and
   on the controller) switches the mode. The PlayStation mode uses the blue theme (`playStationColors`) and its own animated
   background (`PlayStationBackground`: light ribbons and drifting face-button shapes), cross-faded with the user's background
   in 700 ms (instantly with reduced motion). The profile chip sits in the bottom-left corner in both modes; the DELOS logo
   stays top-left.
7. Pairing flow `Screen.PairPs`: Find (search, circles), Sign in (`PsSignInActivity`, Sony's page full screen), PIN (8-digit
   pad for the controller), Done (Play now or back to Start).
8. Streams run in `DelosStreamActivity` behind the `StreamConnection` interface: `NvConnection` for PCs, `PsConnection` for
   consoles (intent extra `PsConnection.EXTRA_CONSOLE`). `PsConnection` wakes the console, retries ctrl while it wakes, and
   feeds the decoder renderer as moonlight-common-c does (parameter sets before each keyframe, unwrapped frame numbers; a
   keyframe request and dropped frames until it arrives on `DR_NEED_IDR` or a full queue). Audio: Opus through `PsOpus`
   (JNI over the bundled libopus, packet-loss concealment) into the Android audio renderer. Input: the controller handler's
   buttons, sticks, triggers, motion (deg/s, m/s² → rad/s, g, orientation integrated from the gyro) and touchpad become the
   console's controller state; rumble, light bar and RTT come back. Keyboard, mouse, clipboard, commands and the runtime
   bitrate are not offered for a console.
9. PlayStation settings (`Screen.PsSettings`, the button under the console circles) are a file of their own (`PsPrefs`,
   `ps_settings`): the classic stream keys plus keyframe-on-loss, video queue depth, audio buffer and swapped channels.
   Defaults on first use: 1080p, 60 fps, HEVC, DELOS's recommended bitrate. Remote Play offers 360p to 1080p at 30 or 60 fps.
10. Bitrate: automatic by default (the console adapts to the reported loss, up to `PsPrefs.autoCeilingKbps`). A fixed
    bitrate is 2–50 Mbit/s, or 2–200 Mbit/s with `ps_unlock_bitrate`; turning the unlock off brings a higher value back to
    50. The slider's track is coloured by resolution: green up to `PsPrefs.idealBitrateKbps` (30 Mbit/s at 1080p), orange up
    to `autoCeilingKbps` (50 Mbit/s at 1080p), red above, with a milestone at each boundary. The figures rest on the
    reported 30–50 Mbit/s of a PS5 at 1080p (protocol spec, section 9, unverified).
11. Console address: every start runs a broadcast search that stops at the paired console's answer (host id = paired MAC), so
    a console that moved to another address (Wi-Fi to Ethernet, a new lease) is found and its address stored.
12. Senkusha (spec 6.4, `PsSenkusha`): UDP 9297, Takion v7 without encryption; protocol request, empty BIG, ten echo pings
    for the round trip, binary searches for the largest packet from (MTU probes) and to (pings with don't-fragment) the
    console. The results go to the launch spec, message fragmentation (the smaller MTU) and don't-fragment on the stream
    socket. Any failure or more than 8 s gives the fallback 1454 / 1 ms; the result is kept per console for the process.
13. Loss recovery (spec 7.3): a frame FEC cannot rebuild makes the feeder drop pictures until a keyframe (an IDR, or a
    picture whose first slice is an I slice), the request repeats every second while it waits, and an adaptive profile
    change (STREAMINFO resolution entries, the frames' adaptive index) switches the parameter sets. Video and audio loss are
    logged every 10 s.
14. Audio buffer: filled to its start depth once, then played continuously; a late unit is waited for one frame and
    concealed, only 200 ms without any unit restarts it. Units are keyed by the 16-bit index, ordered by distance.
15. Silence: 2.5 s without a packet shows the poor-connection notice; at 10 s a new session is opened (find, wake, ctrl,
    stream) for up to 20 s while the stream screen, decoder and audio stay; only then does the stream end.
16. Background: leaving the stream activity without leaving the stream (Home, another app) parks the session
    (`PsSessionPark`) for 5 minutes with its heartbeats and acks running; starting the same console again takes it back
    without a wake or a new session, from a keyframe.
17. DualSense: the console is told the controller is a DualSense, so it sends haptics as voice-coil PCM (audio marker 0x02);
    its mean level per channel drives the two rumble motors. Trigger effects (data type 11) go to `setAdaptiveTriggers`,
    which a wired DualSense driven by `DualSenseController` (USB, "bind all USB" on) applies; PC hosts' 0x5503 effects take
    the same path.
18. Microphone (spec 8.5): the PlayStation setting (Audio tab) asks for the record-audio permission; during a stream the
    voice source at 48 kHz mono goes out as 40-byte Opus frames (constant 32 kbit/s, restricted low delay), each datagram
    with the two frames before it. The PS stream menu's More page mutes it (MIC_TOGGLE).
19. Handheld controls: with a pad that is not a PlayStation pad, Select is the touchpad click and Guide (on the Odin, Back
    held; a tap opens the stream menu) is Create. Fingers on the screen are the touchpad's touches (gestures, no click).
    The Odin's Home button never reaches apps. Rumble on a pad without a vibrator goes to the device (the PS settings default
    `checkbox_vibrate_fallback` on).
20. Gyro aiming (PS settings, Controls): the device's turn added to the right stick, always or while L2 is held. Each axis
    has its own sensitivity, 25–300 % in steps of 5 (`ps_gyro_aim_h` default 100, `ps_gyro_aim_v` default 180). The stream
    menu's More page changes both live; a value changed there is stored for the game in front (`ps_gyro_aim_h_<title id>`,
    `ps_gyro_aim_v_<title id>`) and used when that game runs again, and for every game while no title is known. The title
    comes from the console's discovery answer (`running-app-titleid`), asked at its address when the stream starts and
    every 30 s; the search never wakes a console. Turn rates under 1.5 deg/s, before the sensitivity, move nothing.
21. Bitrate policy: with automatic bitrate and `ps_visible_loss` on (default) the congestion report carries only the loss FEC
    could not repair, so harmless loss does not lower the bitrate.
22. Statistics panel: the console's target bitrate, frames FEC repaired / lost, keyframe waits; frames carry their first
    unit's arrival so the receive-to-decode figures include reassembly.
23. Receive path: the receive thread runs at URGENT_DISPLAY; one AES call per packet keystream on per-thread buffers; the
    MAC checked in place; frames copied once; a table-driven Reed-Solomon decode. Motion-only state at most every 4 ms, the
    button history resent every 200 ms. The 10 s video log counts units that arrived after their short frame (whether a
    reorder window would pay off).
24. Faster start: the start screen searches for the paired consoles; a tap within a minute skips the search. A second
    reconnect in a row steps the session down to 720p.

## Verification

- `PsVectorsTest`: every test vector of the specification (key derivation, registration, ctrl headers, launch-spec
  keystream, ECDH, stream keys and GMACs, FEC, feedback, account id, wake credential, Takion INIT).
- `PsStreamPartsTest`: a real PS5 discovery answer, frame reassembly with a lost source unit recovered by FEC, loss
  ranges, fragmentation, protobuf round trip.
- Bench on the device: `adb shell am start -n io.github.meitoncz.delos.debug/com.limelight.ps.PsDebugActivity`.

### Not verified

- The stream against wired Ethernet; right stick, triggers, touchpad, motion and rumble with a game; HDR; haptics.
- Audio by ear through `PsOpus` (the decoder runs; sound not checked by a person).

## Evidence (2026-11-21)

AYN Odin 2 Portal Max, Android 13, debug build of the branch: the bench's search found `PS5-113` (192.168.0.148, PS5,
status 200, firmware 14230000, target PS5 1.0).

Odin, debug build: the console circle started `DelosStreamActivity` with `PsConnection`; the PS5 delivered HEVC 1080p at
about 60 fps (Qualcomm decoder statistics 26–60 frames/s on Wi-Fi to Wi-Fi), the picture showed the running game, the stream
menu opened, and Disconnect returned to the shell without errors. No controller input was sent.
